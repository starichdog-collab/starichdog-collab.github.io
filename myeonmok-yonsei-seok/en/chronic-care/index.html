Clinic guide/Diabetes and chronic care

Patient guide · Jungnang-gu, Seoul

Preparing for a diabetes, blood pressure or cholesterol visit

If you are following up on a health screening or continuing treatment in Myeonmok-dong, bring dated results and a list of medicines you currently take.

Diabetes and blood glucose

Bring recent glucose or HbA1c results if you have them. If you already keep a glucose diary, mark the time and whether the reading was before or after a meal. Note any questions about your medicines.

High cholesterol and other lipid results

Bring the full lipid report, including LDL, HDL and triglycerides if listed. An older report and your current prescriptions give the clinician something to compare with the new figures.

High blood pressure

If you measure your blood pressure at home, bring your existing readings with dates and times. Ask how to prepare if you need another test. Confirm fasting and medication instructions with the clinic for your appointment.
