Clinic guide/Dialysis in Jungnang-gu

Patient guide · Jungnang-gu, Seoul

Hemodialysis in Jungnang-gu

The clinic’s dialysis unit is at 398 Myeonmok-ro in Myeonmok-dong, near Myeonmok Station. Call before visiting to ask about a first consultation or a transfer.

First visit or transfer →Hours and directions

Which entrance and floor?

Set your map destination to 면목연세석내과 or use the Korean street address below. Outpatient reception is on floor 2 and the dialysis unit occupies floors 5 and 6. When you arrange your visit, ask which floor to go to first.

From Sangbong-dong, Mangu-dong or Yongmasan

Choose your home address or departure station as the starting point on the map. If you start near Yongmasan Station, keep Myeonmok Station as the landmark for the clinic. Ask about drop-off or step-free access if you need it.

Confirming a dialysis appointment

The outpatient timetable does not give dialysis session times. Ask the unit about availability for the date you need, the records it wants to review and when it can confirm an appointment.
