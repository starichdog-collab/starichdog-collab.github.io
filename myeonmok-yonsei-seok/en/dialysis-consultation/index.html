Clinic guide/First visit or transfer

Patient guide · Jungnang-gu, Seoul

Planning a first dialysis consultation or a transfer

When you call, say whether you are seeking an initial kidney consultation or already receive hemodialysis at another unit. This helps reception direct your enquiry.

Before a first consultation

Ask whether a referral, recent test results or a current medication list would help. Confirm the appointment time, the reception floor and how the clinic wants to receive any records.

When changing dialysis units

Ask the receiving clinic when it can review your records and confirm a start date. Coordinate the plan with your current dialysis team before changing an existing appointment.

If someone is coming with you

Tell the clinic if you are bringing a companion, use a wheelchair or need help communicating. Ask about the entrance, movement between floors and whether a companion can join the consultation.
