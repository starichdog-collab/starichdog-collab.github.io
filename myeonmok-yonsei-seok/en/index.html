Patient guide · Jungnang-gu, Seoul

Internal medicine and dialysis in Myeonmok, Seoul

Myeonmok Yonsei Seok is an internal medicine clinic in Jungnang-gu. Outpatient visits take place on the second floor. The dialysis unit is on the fifth and sixth floors of the same building.

Dialysis in Jungnang-gu →Hours and directions

Coming in with a test result?

For a general medical concern or a follow-up for diabetes, high blood pressure or high cholesterol, use the outpatient guide. It explains what to bring and which questions to ask before a test.

Arranging dialysis?

Start with a phone call if this is your first consultation or you receive dialysis elsewhere. Ask about available appointments, records from your current unit and where to check in.
