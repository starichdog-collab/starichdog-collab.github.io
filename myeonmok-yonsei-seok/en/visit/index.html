Patient guide · Jungnang-gu, Seoul

Outpatient hours and directions

The Smart Building stands opposite the Myeonmok Bon-dong Community Service Center. Use the Korean address below in a map app or show it to your driver.

Outpatient hours

Monday–Friday08:30–20:00
Saturday08:30–16:00
Lunch break13:00–14:00
Sundays and public holidaysClosed

These hours are for outpatient appointments. Call to check the registration cutoff or arrange a dialysis session.

Arriving by subway

Use Exit 2 of Myeonmok Station on Line 7 and look for the Smart Building at 398 Myeonmok-ro. If stairs are difficult, check an accessible route from your actual starting point before leaving.

Checking in

General outpatient visits take place on floor 2. For a dialysis consultation or treatment session, ask the clinic where to check in. Check the registration cutoff before travelling close to closing time.
