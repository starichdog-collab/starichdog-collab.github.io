Clinic guide/Internal medicine

Patient guide · Jungnang-gu, Seoul

Seeing an internal medicine doctor in Myeonmok-dong

The outpatient clinic is on the second floor of the Smart Building in Jungnang-gu. It offers internal medicine consultations, including follow-up for chronic conditions.

Bring the reason for your visit

If another clinic or a screening center suggested a follow-up, bring the report and its date. A medication list or a prescription with drug names and doses can help the doctor review your history.

Check before arranging a test

The clinic lists ultrasound and health screening among its services. Ask which test is available for your concern, whether an appointment is needed and what instructions to follow that day.

Finding the right appointment

Tell reception whether you want to discuss symptoms, review test results or continue care. If the appointment is about kidney disease or a dialysis transfer, say so when you call.
